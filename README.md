# Super Index 📈

### Smart Investing Through Personalized Indexing

Super Index is an investor-first indexing platform that creates personalized investment strategies based on an individual's risk appetite. Instead of offering a one-size-fits-all approach, the platform allocates funds across three custom-built indices designed for conservative, moderate, and aggressive investors.

🏆 Top 10 Finalist among 1000+ teams in Vittarth – Pan India Finance Competition.

---

## Problem Statement

Traditional indices such as NIFTY50 and Sensex follow market-cap weighting and fail to address the diverse risk preferences of investors.

Super Index bridges this gap by providing:

- Personalized portfolio allocation
- Risk-based investing
- ESG-integrated screening
- Dynamic rebalancing strategies
- Goal-oriented investment planning

---

## Solution

The platform consists of three investor-centric indices:

### 🛡️ India Stability 30
Designed for conservative investors seeking stability and lower volatility.

**Key Features**
- ESG-integrated stock selection
- Low-beta companies
- High dividend yield focus
- Defensive sector allocation
- Semi-annual rebalancing

---

### ⚖️ GrowSure 30
Designed for moderate-risk investors balancing growth and safety.

**Key Features**
- Sector allocation based on risk-adjusted returns
- ROE, ROCE and Sales Growth filters
- Blend of established market leaders and emerging companies
- Quarterly rebalancing

---

### 🚀 Growth Alpha 30
Designed for aggressive investors targeting maximum long-term returns.

**Key Features**
- High-growth small and mid-cap companies
- Revenue growth and CAGR screening
- Thematic investing approach
- Bi-monthly rebalancing
- High-beta growth opportunities
